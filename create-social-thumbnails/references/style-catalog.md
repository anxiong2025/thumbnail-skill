# Style catalog

Select one primary style ID. Treat each style as a composition system, not a fixed visual to copy. The user's title, real assets, brand, and platform ratio always take priority.

| ID | Style | Best for | Dominant click driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| `S01` | Warm Handwritten Collage / 暖白手写拼贴 | Resources, templates, design packs | Big number + real collage |
| `S02` | Face-led High CTR / 人物高点击 | Personal brands, tutorials, reactions | Face + concise promise |
| `S03` | Clean UI Showcase / 极简 UI 展示 | SaaS, apps, Figma, workflows | One clear product screen |
| `S04` | Dark Tech Neon / 深色科技霓虹 | AI, coding, automation, finance | High-contrast technical proof |
| `S05` | Bold Number List / 大数字清单 | Collections, lists, comparisons | Large number + category |
| `S06` | Before–After Split / 前后对比 | Redesigns, transformations, tests | Visible contrast |
| `S07` | News Commentary / 新闻观点 | Analysis, updates, explainers | Person/topic + strong angle |
| `S08` | Premium Editorial / 高级杂志 | Branding, interviews, thought leadership | Typography + art direction |
| `S09` | Playful Sticker / 活力贴纸 | Lifestyle, education, creator tips | Color + hand-drawn cues |
| `S10` | Product Hero / 产品主视觉 | Launches, reviews, e-commerce | Product + benefit |

## S01 — Warm Handwritten Collage / 暖白手写拼贴

- Background: warm ivory paper with restrained texture.
- Headline: dark navy brush or marker lettering; highlight one number or phrase with coral or blue strokes.
- Proof: real screenshots arranged as a tidy collage in the lower half.
- Transition: background-colored-to-transparent gradient under the title.
- Optional portrait: supplied cutout at lower-right with one blue or white outline.
- Avoid: invented UI, too many stickers, tiny equal-weight screenshots.

## S02 — Face-led High CTR / 人物高点击

- Background: simple blur, gradient, or one supporting proof image.
- Headline: 3–8 words, bold sans serif, one highlighted keyword.
- Subject: supplied portrait occupies 35–50% of canvas; expression remains authentic.
- Effect: one outline or shadow only.
- Avoid: AI substitute faces, decorative clutter, hiding the eyes.

## S03 — Clean UI Showcase / 极简 UI 展示

- Background: white, light gray, or a soft brand gradient.
- Headline: compact, modern sans serif with generous spacing.
- Proof: one dominant real interface plus at most two supporting cards.
- Effect: subtle radius, shadow, and depth; accuracy beats spectacle.
- Avoid: illegible full-page screenshots or fabricated interface text.

## S04 — Dark Tech Neon / 深色科技霓虹

- Background: near-black with restrained blue, cyan, violet, or green glow.
- Headline: condensed or geometric bold type.
- Proof: real dashboard, code, chart, or device screen.
- Effect: controlled edge lighting and one neon accent.
- Avoid: glow on every object, fake statistics, low-contrast body text.

## S05 — Bold Number List / 大数字清单

- Background: simple light or dark field.
- Headline: the number is largest; category or benefit is second.
- Proof: three to six real examples, clearly grouped.
- Effect: underline, circle, or burst behind the number.
- Avoid: multiple competing numbers or long explanatory copy.

## S06 — Before–After Split / 前后对比

- Layout: clean split, slider metaphor, or diagonal division.
- Headline: one transformation claim.
- Proof: two comparable real images at matching scale and crop.
- Effect: directional arrow or clear BEFORE/AFTER labels.
- Avoid: mismatched comparisons or visual manipulation that exaggerates results.

## S07 — News Commentary / 新闻观点

- Background: topical photo or restrained newsroom graphic.
- Headline: a short factual angle, question, or consequence.
- Subject: real speaker or rights-cleared topic image.
- Effect: one color block or breaking-label treatment.
- Avoid: fabricated quotes, misleading expressions, or unsupported claims.

## S08 — Premium Editorial / 高级杂志

- Background: controlled neutral or muted brand color.
- Headline: elegant serif paired with a clean sans serif.
- Subject: high-quality portrait or product crop with deliberate negative space.
- Effect: fine rules, small issue label, subtle grain.
- Avoid: fake magazine logos, excessive text, cheap glow.

## S09 — Playful Sticker / 活力贴纸

- Background: bright color blocks or warm paper.
- Headline: rounded bold or hand-drawn lettering.
- Proof: real subject or product with original doodles, arrows, tape, and stickers.
- Effect: white cutout borders and two or three accent colors.
- Avoid: copyrighted sticker packs or decoration that covers the proof.

## S10 — Product Hero / 产品主视觉

- Background: clean gradient, tabletop scene, or brand-color field.
- Headline: product benefit first, name second.
- Subject: one real product at large scale, with one supporting feature or result.
- Effect: controlled lighting, shadow, and depth.
- Avoid: altering packaging, logos, ports, materials, or product proportions.

## Selection rules

- Use `S01`, `S03`, or `S05` when real screenshots are the main proof.
- Use `S02` or `S07` when a recognizable person is the click driver.
- Use `S04` for technical topics only when the content genuinely supports the aesthetic.
- Use `S06` only when the before and after are comparable and truthful.
- Use `S08` when authority and taste matter more than maximum visual noise.
- Use `S09` for approachable creator content and `S10` for a single product story.

When two styles are requested, choose one as the base and borrow at most one secondary trait. For example: `S01 + S02` means warm handwritten collage with a stronger supplied portrait, not two competing layouts.
